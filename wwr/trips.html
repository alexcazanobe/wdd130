Wild Rapids Adventure

Experience the thrill of Class III-IV rapids in the scenic Mendoza River. Perfect for those seeking excitement with a touch of challenge, this 3-day adventure will take you through some of Argentina’s most breathtaking landscapes.

Patagonia Whitewater Challenge

Get ready for an adrenaline-packed 5-day expedition on the famous Futaleufú River. Known for its powerful Class IV-V rapids, this trip is designed for seasoned rafters looking for the ultimate challenge.

Gentle River Expedition

Ideal for beginners or families, this 2-day journey along the serene Limay River offers a peaceful rafting experience. Enjoy stunning views of Patagonia’s tranquil beauty, perfect for a relaxing adventure.
